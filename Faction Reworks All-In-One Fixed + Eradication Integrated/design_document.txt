[Necron Major Rework]

Necrons will receive a major rework to their Unit Roster and Heroes.

[Design Philosophy]

Necrons are one of the stronger factions in the base game, especially because of their simplistic buildings and resources while also having regeneration on all units.

I discovered the following flaws:

1. Resource economy too simple: Their resource economy is too simple even for Gladius standards. This allows much stronger and specialized builds in comparison to other factions.

2. Summoning Core too dominant: You only need the "summoning core" to have a solid army from early to midgame. There you can build early, mid and lategame infantry, support monsters and even colonizers.

3. Rapid Rise imbalanced: The ability to buy units/buildings for influence only looks at production cost. That means fast-constructing buildings are extremely strong, like the second construction building in a city. This even allows fast city rushing which is faster than all other factions.
________________________________________
[General Changes]

1. Reworked Resources
Energy and Ore are now needed for different things:
• Buildings: Cost Ore and Energy with ore and energy upkeep
• Infantry: Energy with Energy upkeep
• Vehicles: Cost Ore with Ore upkeep
• Flyers: Cost Ore and Energy with ore and energy upkeep
• Heroes and Monsters: Cost Ore and Energy with ore and energy upkeep

You need both resources now but can decide which path you go depending on your starting resource bonuses.

2. Monster Units
Are now built in the Royal Pyramid, which means you will need the hero building to construct colonizer units.

3. Canoptek Reanimators
Are also able to found cities now.

4. Rapid Rise Rebalancing
Is now way more expensive, but the cost will be reduced drastically when a corresponding tech is researched, which means early on you will no longer be able to fast construct buildings. If you tech the corresponding technology, Rapid Rise is a bit cheaper than before.

5. Unit Rework
Some units get reworked to fit a better power curve (but Necrons were already incredibly good).

6. Necrodermis Repair
Cost reduced and fitted to the unit classes:
• Infantry = cheapest
• Vehicles/Monsters = middle ground
• Heroes = expensive

7. New Fortification: Small Gauss Pylon
A smaller midgame version of the Gauss Pylon which excels against flyers and skimmers and can be constructed by the Canoptek Spyder.

Design Philosophy: Necrons will still be a powerhouse but will have to build other buildings than just summoning cores now.
